Live preview — realtime

A cover appears here as soon as you choose it, even before any video is downloaded. As soon as a video downloads it appears inside the cover, Flip flips it instantly, and Speed plays at that speed. Voice change is heard in the final Process preview, because a browser cannot change pitch live.

No cover or video yet. Choose a cover and it appears here immediately, even before downloading a video.

Hide removes the video so only the cover shows, letting you check whether the video sits correctly in the green area. No border is drawn around the video, so nothing hides the edge where video meets cover.

Control the video directly: click the video to select it and show its corners. Drag the video to move it up, down, left or right. Drag a corner to zoom. At 1x zoom the video cannot move, because it already fits exactly — moving only works after zooming, and the video never leaves the green box.
