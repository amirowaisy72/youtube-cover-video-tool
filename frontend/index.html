    What makes a cover work best

    • Size: 1920x1280 is ideal (at least 1280x720), landscape, sharp — not blurry or stretched.
    • Green screen: one large, flat, solid pure-green rectangle with sharp straight edges, facing the camera straight on — not angled, not curved.
    • Nothing on the green: no text, picture, shadow, gradient or object overlapping the green area. Put name plates and text outside the green.
    • Even green size: the green width and height should both be even numbers (for example 1164x662). Odd sizes can fail the video encode.
    • Green must be the biggest green thing: small plants are fine, but no other large green area, or the tool may pick the wrong one.
    • Format: PNG works best; JPG and WebP also work.
    • After upload: click the cover, press “Detect green area”, and check the outline — the video will fill exactly that area.

    Working example: 1920x1280 image, green screen 1164x662 at position (420, 360) — the “Learn With Amir” office cover.

    YouTube channels

    Simple rule: connect each channel once in Step 1, then pick that channel when you upload — the video goes to the channel you picked. No Channel IDs to type, no manual list to manage.

    1

    Connect YouTube — once per channel

    Paste the Client ID and Client Secret from Google Cloud, press Save YouTube credentials, then press Connect YouTube account and sign in with the channel you want. The tool does everything else itself — no terminal, no commands. To add another channel later, press Connect another channel here and choose that channel in Google sign-in. If you do not have the Client ID and Secret yet, follow the Easy setup guide inside this step.

    ★

    Easy setup — just clicks, in order

    One time only. Steps 1–4 are on Google's website. Step 5 is here. No terminal, no commands, no deep settings to find — each button opens the exact page.

    Setup progress: 0 of 5 steps done.

    Open Google Cloud Console
    Open YouTube API page — press Enable
    Open Credentials page
    Open Audience (Test users) page

    On that page: left sidebar → Audience → scroll to Test users → + Add users → your Gmail → Save.

    Stuck? Quick fixes:
    • "Access blocked / Error 403" → you skipped Step 4. Add your Gmail as a test user, then Connect again.
    • No tab opened on Connect → press Connect again, or click the "Click here to sign in with Google" link that appears below it.
    • Channel name shows but Upload is disabled → that name alone does not enable upload. Finish Step 5 (Connect) first.
    • Only the signed-in channel appears automatically → to add another brand channel, press Connect another channel above and choose that channel in Google sign-in. If you connected before the latest update, Disconnect and Connect again to allow the new channel-list permission.
